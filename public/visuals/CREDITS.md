## Five theme photographs

The `scene-*.webp` landscapes are cropped from real photographs and bundled under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Each has a 2880 × 1512 wide image, a 1440 × 756 compact image and a 420 × 221 preview.

- Forest uses Chris Linnett’s photograph of fog settling among the evergreens in Bergün, Switzerland. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/dark-evergreen-forest-shrouded-in-thick-fog-BoezF2tPJbI)
- Iceberg uses Filippo Salvioni’s photograph of an arched iceberg near Ilulissat, Greenland. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-massive-arch-shaped-iceberg-floats-in-the-cold-ocean-aiADDysGH7M)
- Grassland uses Ollie Singleton’s photograph of rolling fields near Gubbio, Italy. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/rolling-green-hills-with-trees-and-distant-mountains-thtjda3CDRY)
- Rift uses Nick Da Fonseca’s aerial photograph of orange rock and a dry riverbed in Utah’s Valley of the Gods. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/an-aerial-view-of-a-canyon-in-the-desert-vosVDyzZJC0)
- Ocean uses Iain’s aerial photograph of waves breaking over a submerged reef. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/ocean-waves-crashing-against-dark-rocks-naVr1Z6xM3E)

## Animated sign-in scenes

The five `auth-*.webp` backgrounds are original illustrations revised with the built-in ImageGen editor. The brief used matte gouache, a visible paper grain, and lighting tied to each scene. The 1120 × 1400 images depict a forest railway archive, a reading room cut into glacial ice, a country post office, a canyon footbridge, and a submerged observatory. Animation, input, and lighting are rendered by the site.
